BioDex

Design board · v4 · 2026-09-02 · companion to DESIGN.md · the shipped regional dex is the Pacific USA BioDex

A personal Pokédex for real living things. One regional dex — the Pacific USA BioDex — holds 120 animals and 80 plants, counted separately, grouped by ecosystem. A species unlocks when you register a photo of it from your gallery — the photo is linked, never copied, and there is deliberately no in-app identification. Plants carry their uses (edible, medicinal, which part, when), filterable from the chip row. Species you photograph that aren't in the catalogue become your own entries, auto-populated from public sources. Seven frames below — static mockups, not a working app. Animal and plant art is placeholder SVG standing in for photos.

Dex Grid

Home: the product name, the region pill, one meter per kingdom. Animals then plants in one grid; kingdom, use, class and ecosystem chips all compose. User-added species trail below the fold.
