Animal Dex

Design board · v3 · 2026-09-01 · companion to DESIGN.md

A personal Pokédex for real animals, Pacific region. The catalogue ships with the app, grouped by ecosystem; a species unlocks when you register a photo of it from your gallery — the photo is linked, never copied, and there is deliberately no in-app identification. Species you photograph that aren't in the catalogue become your own entries, auto-populated from public sources. Six frames below — static mockups, not a working app. Animal art is placeholder SVG standing in for photos.

Dex Grid

Home, headed by the region. Caught species show your photo; uncaught are silhouettes. Ecosystem chips filter; user-added species trail the catalogue below the fold.
